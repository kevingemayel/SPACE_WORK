Who We Are

Leaders in the façade industry.

We provide solutions for spectacular building skins and innovative façade envelopes. From intelligent planning to efficient installation, ALGECO provides everything necessary to complete the most ambitious architectural designs.

Our name says it all — Aluminium, Glass, Steel, Engineering & Contracting. For more than two decades we have engineered building envelopes that perform, endure and inspire, working hand-in-hand with our clients from the earliest design stages to the most cost-effective outcome.

How It's Made

One team, the full envelope lifecycle.

From conception to handover — design, engineering, fabrication, installation and commissioning delivered entirely in-house.

01 Design & EngineeringSite analysis, concept design, structural analysis and shop drawings — iterated until total client satisfaction is achieved. → 02 FabricationAluminium, metal and composite fabrication on CNC machinery with five-axis robotic precision and micro-tolerance cutting. → 03 InstallationMaterial handling, vertical transport, fixing and waterproofing — elevating your expectations on every floor. → 04 Testing & CommissioningWater penetration, air infiltration, anchor pull-out and acoustic testing to certify safety and design performance. →
