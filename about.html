Who We Are

Driven by a motivation for success.

We provide solutions for spectacular building skins and innovative façade envelopes. From intelligent planning to efficient installation, ALGECO provides everything necessary to complete ambitious architectural designs.

Our team has expanded over many years into a complete discipline of designers, architects, engineers, project managers, foremen and skilled labourers. Together they have successfully completed major projects ranging from business centres and towers to malls, hotels and cultural landmarks.

Today we are currently sub-contracting most of the major projects in Lebanon, with completed and ongoing work across the Middle East and Africa — ready to execute anywhere in the MENA region.
