How It's Made

From conception to reality.

We design the building envelope, engineer and order materials, program our machines to manufacture, install every component, and finally test for performance — all under one roof.

02 — Fabrication

Shaping the future with the latest technology.

Design for manufacturability is the practice of designing products so they are easy to manufacture. Our implementation differs widely — we trust the machines more than humans when it comes to accuracy and precision.

  • ↳ Programming — digital controls operate within micro-tolerances
  • ↳ Cutting — double mitre saw with digital cutting controls
  • ↳ Milling — SBZ fully-automated robotic arm, five-axis technology
  • ↳ Notching & Crimping — variable-angle cuts and seamless corners
  • ↳ Machine Routing — a perfect fit for every accessory
  • ↳ Metal & Composite — full fabrication of cladding and ACP

04 — Testing & Commissioning

Certified safe. Certified to perform.

Testing and commissioning is carried out on every installation to ensure it is safe and meets the design requirements. We pioneered acoustic mock-up testing on full-size specimens using site standards.

AStatic Water Penetration · ASTM E 1105Minimum differential pressure of 20% of inward-acting wind-load design pressure (≥ 300 Pa).
BAnchor Bolt Pull-OutConfirms the anchors that transfer impact and wind loads to the structure perform under a calibrated tension load.
CAir Infiltration · ASTM E 783Tested at 1.5× the laboratory rate, not exceeding 2.25 L/s per m² of fixed wall area.
DAcoustic TestingFull-size mock-up testing under real site conditions — superior to small-sample lab tests.
EPendulum Balustrade TestAn inexpensive, vital check on critical guard-rail components in public areas.
FSite Hose Test · AAMA 501.2A quick check on a system's vulnerability to leakage at the joints.
