What We Do

The building envelope, in every form.

We are experts in windows, doors, curtain walls, shopfronts, balustrades, spider systems, louvers, steel structures, cladding, composite panels, membranes and double-skin envelopes.

01

Curtain Walls

An outer covering in which the outer walls are non-structural — keeping the weather out and the occupants in. We deliver both stick construction, assembled on site with mullions and transoms supporting glass, spandrel and metal panels, and unitised construction, where large units are assembled in our factory, shipped and erected complete with glazing and infill panels.

  • Stick systems
  • Unitised systems
  • Spandrel & brise-soleil
  • Spider / point-fixed glass
02

Casement Doors & Windows

Elements attached to their frame by one or more side hinges, used singly or in pairs within a common frame. Top-hinged units form awning windows and bottom-hinged units form hoppers. Our major system providers for these systems are Technal and Gutmann.

  • Casement
  • Awning
  • Hopper
  • Shopfronts
03

Steel Structures

Structural steel components connected to carry loads and provide full rigidity, fabricated to a specific shape and chemical composition to suit each project's specifications. Steel offers strength and durability, light weight, fast installation, fire resistance, weather resistance and outstanding cost-effectiveness.

  • Structural steel
  • Glass elevators
  • Canopies
  • Feature frames
04

Double-Skin Façades

A system of two skins placed so that air flows in the intermediate cavity. Ventilation of the cavity can be natural, fan-supported or mechanical. The second skin also provides shade from direct sunlight without disrupting the view from inside — a hallmark of high-performance, sustainable architecture.

  • Naturally ventilated
  • Mechanically ventilated
  • Solar shading
05

Exterior Metal Cladding

The top finishes for a striking façade — aluminium for a lightweight yet strong skin; zinc for versatile, low-maintenance contemporary architecture; copper for a timeless patina; corten for weather-resistant character; and corrugated steel for durable, expressive texture.

  • Aluminium
  • Zinc
  • Copper
  • Corten
  • Corrugated steel
06

Aluminium Composite Panels

Flat panels of two thin coil-coated aluminium sheets bonded to a non-aluminium core. ACPs are frequently used for external cladding and façades, insulation and signage — delivering a flawless, flat finish across large surfaces.

  • External cladding
  • Signage
  • Insulated panels
07

Louvers

A sustainable way to cut heating and cooling costs and an effective system for controlling a building's climate — guiding fresh air in while ushering stale air out. Designed to fit homes, commercial structures and industrial constructions, with options in size, shape, material and finish. Aluminium louvers are low-cost and bring numerous benefits.

  • Climate control
  • Ventilation
  • Aluminium finishes
08

Internal Partitions

Glazed partitions are a common feature of interior design for offices, commercial premises and public buildings — creating distinct but open work areas, enclosing spaces without restricting light, forming entrances and adding a special design feature to any interior.

  • Office pods
  • Glazed walls
  • Entrances
